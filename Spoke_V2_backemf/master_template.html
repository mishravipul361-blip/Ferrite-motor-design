4Root-cause analysis

Root causes of every mismatch, in order of their effect on the efficiency map.

main causeEddy-current loss formula missing 2π²

Motor-CAD's Steinmetz model is Kh·f·B^α + 2π²·Keddy·f²·B². The earlier FEMM model used Keddy·f²·B², which made eddy loss about 20× too small. Eddy loss grows with f², so the error grew with speed. That is why the earlier high-speed efficiency was 1.5–3 points too high.

Proof: your loss chart. At 2500 Hz and 0.5 T the formula with 2π² gives 83 W/kg, against about 85 W/kg plotted; without 2π² it gives 18.5 W/kg.

cause 2Iron loss from one field snapshot at the fundamental frequency

The earlier model took B at 4 rotor positions, assumed a sinusoid at the electrical frequency, and estimated rotor loss from slot ripple. That misses (a) how iron loss grows with load and field weakening, and (b) the rotor iron loss, which comes only from slot and stator-MMF harmonics. Rotor loss came out 93 % too low (5 W against 44 W at 4000 rpm).

numericalFEMM remeshing between rotor positions

With mi_moverotate, FEMM builds a new mesh at every position, and only about 30 % of elements stay in place. Element-wise flux density then jumps between positions, and the jumps look like harmonics. The n²-weighted eddy term amplifies them, so the first full-cycle attempt gave about 2× stator and 10–25× rotor loss. Fixed with FEMM's air-gap element, which keeps the mesh constant.

model choiceHow harmonics are counted

With clean fields, counting every stator harmonic gives +94 % stator loss: the 9th/11th yoke harmonics add eddy loss that Motor-CAD's numbers do not contain. Peak-B Steinmetz in the stator (+11 %) and per-harmonic Steinmetz in the rotor (−12 %) reproduce Motor-CAD best. Motor-CAD's exact internal option is not visible from the exports (item 7.3 would confirm it).

back-EMF stageMaterial curve and sampling

The +0.6 % back-EMF offset came from the typical B-H curve; Motor-CAD's own curve removed it. The THD and cogging gaps came from different time resolution, not from different physics. Sampled the same way, FEMM's THD is 5.52 % against Motor-CAD's 5.42 %.

no change neededWhat was right from the start

Geometry (yoke mass exact), winding (R, MLT, turns, kw1 exact), d-q flux linkage and torque (1–2 %), DC copper and mechanical loss (exact), AC copper loss (±9 %, no bias), drive limits and MTPA control.
