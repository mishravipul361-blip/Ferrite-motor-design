FEMM 2D geometric sensitivity · 22 designs + 3 candidates · about 950 solves · tuned to Motor-CAD

Spoke V2 sensitivity study and design recommendation

Which geometric parameter affects which performance figure, and by how much. The aim is to maximise torque at 2000 rpm / 360 A and minimise loss at 9339 rpm / 3.24 N·m, while keeping back-EMF THD below 5 % and torque ripple below 10 % at every operating point, within the hard constraints shaft OD = 30 mm, bridge ≥ 1.0 mm and bridge offset > 3.5 mm.

1Problem set-up

Objective 1 · maximiseTorque at 2000 rpm, 360 A rms

MTPA electromagnetic torque at 100 °C. Current angle swept 17° / 23° / 29°, parabola peak, averaged over 2 rotor positions.

Objective 2 · minimiseTotal loss at 9339 rpm, 3.24 N·m

Field-weakening point at the 49.4 V limit (52 V, Mi 0.95), from a local d-q model (4 solves). Loss = Cu DC + Cu AC + iron + mechanical, using the models tuned to Motor-CAD.

Requirement · THD < 5 %No-load back-EMF THD

20 °C, 30 points per cycle, harmonics 2–13: the Motor-CAD definition (Motor-CAD's original design gives 5.42 %).

Requirement · ripple < 10 %At every operating point

Worst full-load point found from Motor-CAD's envelope: 9000 rpm, maximum current (OP1, 17.6 %). Checked for every design. Light load (OP2, 3.24 N·m) and peak torque (OP3) were also checked for the candidates.

Hard constraintsShaft 30 mm · bridge ≥ 1.0 mm · offset > 3.5 mm

Applied to every design D00–D20 and to the candidates. R00 (Motor-CAD original, shaft 25 mm) is a reference only. R00 (shaft 25 mm): torque 45.73 N·m, loss 685.8 W, THD 5.31 %, ripple 16.1 %.

MethodOne at a time, then combined

10 parameters, each at a low and a high value around D00 (bridge upwards only). Combined candidates were chosen from the sensitivities and verified with full FEMM solves. Skew was evaluated from the computed waveform harmonics.

2Recommendation

Recommended: candidate C3 with a 6° (mechanical) 3-step rotor skew. It meets every requirement, keeps SWG 11 and the 16 A/mm² limit, and improves the high-speed loss without losing torque.
  • Geometry: slot opening 5 → 4 mm, tooth tip depth 0.8 → 1.2 mm, magnet inset 2 → 1.5 mm. Tooth width stays 15.5 mm, shaft 30 mm, bridge 1.0 mm, bridge offset 4.0 mm. Everything else as Motor-CAD.
  • Winding (unchanged): SWG 11 (2.9464 mm), 20 conductors per slot, 10 turns per coil, 2 parallel paths. Current density at 360 A is 15.24 A/mm² rms (< 16). Slot area 271.1 mm², wire fill 0.532 (baseline 0.518, +2.7 %).
  • Skew: 3 axial steps of 2° (6° in total, one cogging period), using the 3 existing magnet segments.
  • Result with skew: torque 45.7 N·m (46.14 without skew; D00 45.50); OP2 loss 644 W (−4.5 %); THD 2.3 %; ripple OP1 / OP2 / OP3 1.4 / 1.9 / 2.0 %.
  • Why not C1: C1 gives about 0.6 N·m more torque, but only through the 16.5 mm tooth. With SWG 11 fixed that pushes the wire fill to 0.562 (0.65 of the winding area), which is very likely not windable. C2 has the same problem.
  • Without skew, no geometry change brings light-load ripple below 10 %. At 3.24 N·m the ripple is almost entirely cogging, which only a skew of one cogging period cancels.

3Which parameter affects what

Reading the matrix
  • Win-win parameters (both objectives improve together): smaller slot opening (+0.9 % torque, −5.3 % loss per −1 mm), deeper tooth tip (+0.7 %, −3.6 % per +0.4 mm), smaller magnet opening (+0.7 %, −4.8 % per −1 mm). These change the air-gap field shape and leakage, not the magnet flux.
  • Trade-off parameters (torque against loss, through back-EMF): Magnet_Y, magnet depth, bridge thickness, magnet inset. Anything that lowers back-EMF reduces field-weakening current and iron loss at 9339 rpm, but costs torque at 2000 rpm. About −3.5 % torque buys −9 to −11 % OP2 loss.
  • Minor parameters: Magnet_Z, bridge offset (±0.5 %), tooth width (torque ±1.5 %, loss ±1 %).
  • THD: improved by smaller magnet inset (4.36 % at 1.5 mm) and a deeper tooth tip (4.81 %); worsened by a smaller magnet opening (6.19 %). The slot opening has an optimum near 5 mm (both 4 and 6 mm are worse).
  • Ripple at the worst point: improved by a smaller slot opening (16 → 9.0 %) and a smaller magnet inset (9.2 %); worsened by a deeper tip (20 %), a larger inset (21 %) and a larger slot opening (25 %).

4Trade-off between the two objectives

Most single parameters move the design along one trade-off line (more back-EMF gives more torque and more high-speed loss). The win-win parameters move it off that line, up and to the left. C1, C2 and C3 combine them and sit above and left of D00. C3 has the lowest loss and keeps the original tooth width, so the SWG 11 winding still fits.

5Skew: the lever for THD and light-load ripple

  • Cogging repeats every 6° (12 slots, 10 poles). A skew of one cogging period (6°) cancels it, which is why light-load ripple (OP2) drops from about 95 % to under 2 %. That matches the "optimum skewing angle 6°" in Motor-CAD's output.
  • Cost: the fundamental skew factor at 6° mech (30° el) is 0.989, so torque and back-EMF fall by about 1.1 %. A 3-step skew (using the 3 existing magnet segments) gives almost the same benefit as continuous skew at 6°.
  • 2D multi-slice approximation: axial inter-slice leakage and end effects are not modelled. Confirm with Motor-CAD's skew option (E-Magnetics › Skew, 3 steps, 6°).

Accuracy and limits
  • FEMM model tuned to Motor-CAD: torque within about 1 %, back-EMF within 0.5 %, efficiency within about 0.6 pt. Differences between designs are more accurate than absolute values.
  • OP2 loss uses a local d-q model (4 solves). Iron loss is scaled from the tuned model and AC copper loss uses the hybrid estimate (×2), so trends are reliable to roughly ±2 %.
  • Ripple uses 12 rotor positions over the 12° period (1° steps). THD uses Motor-CAD's 30-point definition. Absolute ripple values can differ from Motor-CAD by about 5–25 % (see the operating-point report).
  • Wire is fixed at SWG 11 with 20 conductors per slot, so resistance and current density (15.24 A/mm² rms at 360 A) are identical in every design. Slot area and wire fill are in the Excel sheet Designs: C3 0.532, C1 0.562, C2 0.551, baseline 0.518.
