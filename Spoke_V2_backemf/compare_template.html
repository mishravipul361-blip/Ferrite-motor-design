What this comparison can and cannot show

The FEMM no-load run turned the rotor in 2° steps. Folded into the 6° cogging period, that gives only three distinct positions (0, 2 and 4°), each solved 12 times. The 12 repeats agree, which confirms the mesh is consistent from one rotor step to the next. The three points sit on the Motor-CAD curve once the origin is shifted by __C_SH__°.

FEMM's pk-pk value from those three points (__COG_F__ N·m) is a lower bound, not a measurement. Motor-CAD gives 0.663 N·m (VW) and 0.731 N·m (CE). A dedicated FEMM cogging sweep at 0.1–0.2° steps (about 10 min) is the next step if cogging needs tuning.

5What the Motor-CAD outputs show about its setup

  • Delta connection. Line-line EMF equals phase EMF (8.213 V), "Max line-line / phase voltage ratio" is 1, and line-line resistance is ⅔ of phase (3.399 vs 5.098 mΩ). FEMM's phase result applies directly.
  • Magnet and winding temperature 20 °C. The conductor temperature reads 20 °C and the phase resistance of 5.098 mΩ equals the 20 °C value. The EMF level agrees with FEMM's Br of 0.46 T.
  • End-winding MLT factor 0.7 (108 mm calculated → 75.62 mm used) and end-winding inductance factor 0.7. These are user settings that change resistance and inductance, not back-EMF. FEMM's resistance uses the same 75.62 mm, which is why it matches exactly.
  • Stacking factor 0.97 on stator and rotor. This is the value FEMM uses, and it was confirmed independently from the lamination masses.
  • Phase advance 78.17° and drive offset 75° el. These do not act at open circuit (I = 0), and the d/q flux linkages are pure magnet flux (ψd = 22.05 mVs, ψq ≈ 0).
  • Air-gap graph labelled "on load" with zero current is the same as the open-circuit field.
  • Output values that are zero (rotor inertia, magnet and rotor weight, Ld/Lq) come from the adaptive rotor template and a no-load-only run. They are expected and do not indicate an error.

6How the Motor-CAD curves were digitised

  • Each photo was corrected for camera perspective with a projective transform fitted to the four plot-frame corners. Each corner was located against its axis labels (for example ±12 V and 0/340° el for the back-EMF graph).
  • Curve pixels are pixels that are darker or more saturated than the local background. Text, grid lines and screen moiré were removed by stroke-shape filtering.
  • Back-EMF: the three phase curves were traced through their crossings, then folded onto phase 1 using the 120° shifts and half-wave symmetry, giving a full 0–360° curve from the visible 0–345°.
  • Accuracy check against Motor-CAD's own numbers: rms __RMS_M__ vs 8.213 V (+0.15 %), peak __PK_M__ vs 11.51 V (+0.3 %), fundamental __F1_M__ vs 11.60 V (+0.15 %), cogging pk-pk 0.662 vs 0.663 N·m (−0.15 %). THD reads lower (__THD_M__ vs 5.42 %) because the photo cannot resolve harmonics above about the 20th.
  • Exporting the curves from Motor-CAD (Graphs › Data) would remove this step and the remaining ±0.15 V / ±0.01 T uncertainty.
