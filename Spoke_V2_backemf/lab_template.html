THD and cogging depend on sampling

FEMM is solved every 2° el (EMF) and 0.1–0.2° mech (cogging). Sampled the way Motor-CAD's curves appear to be (about 30 points per electrical cycle, forward difference), FEMM's THD becomes {{V_THD30}} % against Motor-CAD's 5.42 %. Motor-CAD's cogging curve is smooth with rounded peaks, which suggests it too is coarsely sampled. The "points per cycle" settings in Motor-CAD's E-Magnetics calculation tab will confirm this. Fillets (0.3 / 1.0 / 1.95 mm) and a 4× finer air-gap mesh each change FEMM cogging by under 2 %.

Limits of this study
  • 2D model: no 3D end effects of the spoke rotor (3D torque is typically a few % lower).
  • AC copper loss uses the low-frequency round-wire formula. It agrees with Motor-CAD's AC loss within about ±9 % up to 9000 rpm, with no bias. Above 9000 rpm, where there is no Motor-CAD data, it may be overstated, because the wire is 1.2 skin depths across at 1 kHz.
  • Iron loss, checked against Motor-CAD's map: stator about +11 %, rotor about −12 %, total +7 % on average. Minor loops (Kc 0.65) are not modelled separately.
  • The d-q map has 6 × 6 current points with cubic interpolation, against Motor-CAD's 30-point saturation model.
