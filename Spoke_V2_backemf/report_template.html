9Comparing with Motor-CAD

E(n, T) = __V_E_PH_RMS__ V × (n / 1000 rpm) × [1 − 0.0018 × (Tmagnet − 20)] phase rms
ELL(n, T) = __V_E_LL_RMS__ V × (n / 1000 rpm) × [1 − 0.0018 × (Tmagnet − 20)] line-line rms, star
Set Motor-CAD the same way before comparing
  • Magnet temperature 20 °C in the E-Magnetics calculation (or scale with the formula above).
  • Compare the same quantity: phase or line-line, rms or peak, star or delta.
  • Expect FEMM to be within about 1–3 %. The remaining differences come from the M235-35A B-H curve, the unmodelled fillets (slot corner 1.95 mm; pocket, magnet and lamination fillets) and mesh density.

Not modelled

  • Fillet radii (slot corner, pocket, magnet, lamination) and the rotor lamination inset parameter.
  • 3D end effects: axial leakage of the spoke rotor and end-winding flux. 2D and Motor-CAD 2D FEA both leave these out.
  • Magnet non-linearity near the knee (it is not reached at no load).
