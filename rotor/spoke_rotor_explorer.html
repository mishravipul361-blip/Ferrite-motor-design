Point construction

All points are built on the left half (x ≤ 0) of a sector centred on 90°, then mirrored. θ = pole_pitch_deg, t = Bridge_thickness/2.

The web trick. The pole-piece axis at 90° + θ/2 is the line x = −y·tan(θ/2). A parallel line shifted inward by a perpendicular distance t has a horizontal shift of t / cos(θ/2). P and G sit on that shifted line at y = r_im and y = r_s + bridge_offset. The neighbouring sector mirrors it on the other side of the axis, so the iron web between two inner pockets is exactly 2t = Bridge_thickness.

Glue gap (added, not in the script). The slot P–Q–R stays where the script puts it, so the lamination is unchanged. The magnet is the slot offset inward by magnet_glue_gap on the two side faces only. The top stays at r_om in contact with the pole-piece lips, and the floor stays on r_im facing the air pocket. P_m, Q_m, R_m are where the offset side lines meet r_im, each other and r_om. At 0 the magnet equals the slot, exactly like the script.

Consequence: the magnet floor width 2|P_x| is not an input. It is set by pole_number, r_im and Bridge_thickness.
