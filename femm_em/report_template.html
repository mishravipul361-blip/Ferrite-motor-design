Local irreversible demagnetisation is predicted at the magnet tips at rated current. Ferrite HcJ also falls in the cold (≈ −0.2 to −0.3 %/K), so a cold start at full current is the worst case. Options: a higher-HcJ ferrite grade, thicker pole-piece lips over the magnet top, a smaller magnet opening, or a lower peak current.

Limitations

what a 2D magnetostatic model leaves out
  • End-winding leakage inductance (end turns ≈ ⅓ of the wire length) is not in Ld/Lq: real base speed will be a few hundred rpm lower.
  • 3D end effects of the ferrite spoke rotor (axial leakage) typically cost 5–15 % torque. The calibration to Motor-CAD absorbs part of this only if Motor-CAD included it.
  • Iron loss is estimated, not solved per element over time; AC copper and magnet eddy losses are omitted.
  • Thermal: 360 A rms is ≈15.8 A/mm² in the wire, a short-time rating. Continuous capability needs a thermal model.
  • Mechanical: rotor bridge stress at 12 000 rpm is not checked.
