Back-EMF validation: FEMM against Motor-CAD

Verified. Back-EMF amplitude, fundamental, rms, Ke and magnet flux agree with Motor-CAD within 0.7 %. Air-gap, tooth and back-iron flux densities agree within 1 %. THD is 1.48 % against 1.25 %: 0.23 points higher, in the 9th to 13th harmonics that the rotor skew suppresses.

How the comparison was made

FEMM model
2D magnetostatic, full machine, geometry from the Design9795_1 screenshots (slot area 281.2 mm² and wire fill 0.5129, both exactly as Motor-CAD). Stack 64 mm, DM4654 magnets at 100 °C (Br 0.394 T).
Rotor positions
1° mechanical steps over half an electrical cycle (36 solves); the second half follows from half-wave symmetry, giving 72 points per electrical cycle.
Skew
Motor-CAD rotor skew: 3 equal slices at −2 / 0 / +2° mechanical. FEMM is 2D, so the phase flux linkage is averaged over the three shifted rotor positions, exactly, on its Fourier series.
EMF and THD
Resampled at 30 points per cycle with a forward difference, like Motor-CAD's back-EMF calculation (30 points per cycle, 1 cycle). THD uses harmonics 2 to 15.

Motor-CAD reads the tooth tip and rotor back iron at its own fixed points, so those two cannot be compared one-to-one with section averages.

What this means for the Lab model

  • The magnet flux, which sets torque per amp and the field-weakening speed, matches Motor-CAD to 0.28 %.
  • Motor-CAD includes the rotor skew in all its results. The FEMM Lab model is built in 2D, so at the same current its torque will be about 1 % higher (fundamental skew factor 0.990) and its torque ripple higher, unless the same skew correction is applied. The Lab comparison will show both.
